# Taiwan Equity Lens v0.53.0

Release date: 2026-07-17

v0.53.0 turns the post-v0.50 Industry Trend foundation into a fuller Market Intelligence research surface. The official importer and price/news/flow map now support a deterministic industry sentiment composite, retained daily history, explainable cycle phase, experimental projection, and peak/trough risk diagnostics.

This release remains research workflow support. Current sentiment is a descriptive composite. Projection is deterministic and experimental. Peak/trough values are risk diagnostics, not probabilities, price targets, or exact dates.

## What changed after v0.50.0

v0.50.0 introduced the deterministic Industry Trend Report. The intervening Market Intelligence/importer foundation added official TWSE/TPEx company identity and price history, mapped news and institutional flow, independent freshness gates, partial-source reporting, and an enriched research universe. v0.53.0 builds on that foundation rather than replacing it:

- `industry-sentiment-v1` combines news `0.40`, price `0.30`, and institutional flow `0.30`.
- Every component and composite score is clamped to `[-100, 100]`; only displayed values are rounded to one decimal place.
- A numeric composite requires at least two fresh usable components. Exactly two are renormalized, marked `partial`, and cannot receive `high` confidence.
- Missing, stale, invalid, and source-error inputs remain explicit. They are never silently changed to zero or neutral evidence.
- The report exposes labels, warming/cooling change, coverage-based confidence, cycle phase, reasons, warnings, component contributions, and methodology version.
- One stable row per `(as_of_date, category, methodology_version)` is retained in `industry_sentiment_history.csv`; same-key reruns replace the row deterministically.
- Runtime features use prior/current snapshots only. Backtest-only future peak/trough labels remain isolated in validation.

## Deterministic inputs

News uses a versioned Traditional Chinese finance lexicon and deterministic longest-match-first tokenization. Title/summary weights are `0.65/0.35`; negation spans three tokens; intensity is capped at `2.0`; recency half-lives are 3 days for 5D and 7 days for 20D. A source can use at most `40%` of uncapped news weight. Clipped weight stays neutral and unallocated rather than being redistributed.

Price combines return, positive-return breadth, and 5D volume confirmation with weights `0.60/0.25/0.15`. Return scales are `8%` for 5D and `15%` for 20D.

Institutional flow divides normalized net shares by matching traded shares, then combines ratio/persistence with weights `0.75/0.25` and ratio scale `0.05`. Joins use only matching `(date, stock_id)` rows with positive traded-share volume. A valid session requires at least `60%` coverage; 5D needs at least 3 valid sessions and 20D needs at least 10.

Official flow-history requests use:

```text
TWSE: GET https://www.twse.com.tw/rwd/zh/fund/T86
      date=YYYYMMDD, selectType=ALLBUT0999, response=json
TPEx: POST https://www.tpex.org.tw/www/zh-tw/insti/dailyTrade
      type=Daily, sect=EW, date=YYYY/MM/DD, response=json
```

The importer seeks 20 sessions per available market, skips non-trading dates, deduplicates `(date, stock_id, source)`, and retains partial errors per market/date.

## Confidence and history gates

Confidence reflects freshness and coverage, not score magnitude. Price and flow are fresh for the latest completed Taiwan market session or the immediately preceding expected session. News is fresh when a mapped article exists in the previous 48 hours. Holidays do not create staleness by themselves.

- `high`: all three components are fresh, at least five 5D articles, at least `80%` price coverage, and at least four valid 5D flow sessions.
- `medium`: at least two components are fresh and no required component has a source error.
- `low`: two components are usable but weak coverage or a partial-source warning exists.
- No confidence is emitted below two usable components.

Forecast is suppressed below 20 valid snapshots. From 20-59 snapshots, the output can show experimental 1D/5D deterministic projection without a turning window. At 60 or more, it can also show 0-100 peak/trough risk diagnostics and an experimental `1_to_3_days`, `4_to_7_days`, or `unclear` category. `calibrated_probability` remains `null`.

## Run the workflow

```powershell
python -m taiwan_stock_analysis.cli research market-intelligence research.csv --industry-trend-report research-dist/industry-trends/industry_trend_report.json --news-csv market_news.csv --fund-flow-csv fund_flow.csv --as-of 2026-07-17T18:00:00+08:00 --output-dir research-dist/market-intelligence
python -m taiwan_stock_analysis.cli research sentiment-backtest research-dist/market-intelligence/industry_sentiment_history.csv --output research-dist/market-intelligence/sentiment_backtest_report.json
```

Run the bundled offline path and release checks:

```powershell
python -m taiwan_stock_analysis.cli demo quickstart --output-dir .tmp-v053-demo
python -m taiwan_stock_analysis.cli doctor demo --output-dir .tmp-v053-demo
python -m taiwan_stock_analysis.cli research sentiment-backtest .tmp-v053-demo/market-intelligence/industry_sentiment_history.csv --output .tmp-v053-demo/market-intelligence/sentiment_backtest_report.json
python -m taiwan_stock_analysis.cli doctor release --version 0.53.0
```

`doctor demo` verifies the offline artifact set, sentiment history, report fields, and dashboard hooks without modifying output. The short demo history should produce an experimental report with failed minimum-history checks. `doctor release` verifies local release metadata and documentation; it does not verify external source availability.

## Shadow validation and promotion

Validation uses expanding-window walk-forward evaluation by date. Random train/test splits are prohibited. A valid backtest exits successfully even when its promotion checks fail. Runtime status remains experimental even if `promotion_ready` becomes true; promotion requires a separate human-reviewed model-validity decision.

All gates must pass:

- at least 252 market sessions;
- at least 30 peak and 30 trough events;
- no look-ahead or revised-input leakage;
- Brier score better than the unconditional event-rate baseline for both targets;
- precision and recall reported at risk thresholds 50 and 70;
- stability in two non-overlapping chronological holdouts;
- a written model-validity report.

Thresholds are not tuned merely to obtain a passing result.

## Future LLM reviewer boundary

`NewsSentimentReviewer` is a separate typed protocol only. v1 has no default reviewer, provider SDK, API-key access, or runtime LLM call. A future reviewer may return a separate `llm_review` payload, but it cannot overwrite `deterministic_score` without an explicit, tested promotion policy in a later version.

## Live-readiness boundary

Do not call a live run ready because files were created. Inspect partial market/date failures, per-market session counts, joined traded shares, industry coverage, freshness, warnings, component status, and confidence. Each industry must have a numeric score or explicit `insufficient_data`; neither outcome is investment advice or a forecast of returns.
