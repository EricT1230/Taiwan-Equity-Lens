# v0.52.0 Official Market Data Importer Design

## Status

Implemented on `codex/v0.51.0-market-intelligence-map` as the next stage of the Market Intelligence plan.

## Context

The v0.51 Market Intelligence Map could combine news, institutional flow, and an Industry Trend Report, but its price-history input was still a local CSV. Live validation correctly blocked the combined report because the bundled price snapshot was stale. The next milestone must refresh prices and official industry identity for both listed and OTC research stocks without changing the existing trend-report contract.

## Decision

Add a deterministic `market_data_importer` module and `research market-data` command that:

- resolves research stocks against TWSE and TPEx official company profiles,
- joins official industry codes to official industry names,
- fetches monthly daily-price history from TWSE `STOCK_DAY` or TPEx `tradingStock`,
- normalizes ROC dates, close prices, and volume into the existing `industry_price_history.csv` schema,
- fetches and filters TWSE T86 plus TPEx three-institution daily flow,
- writes an enriched `research_official.csv` while preserving user research fields,
- leaves a non-empty custom category unchanged unless `--replace-category` is explicit,
- writes a traceable market-data quality report and exposes it in the dashboard,
- lets `research run --fetch-market-data` feed the refreshed files directly into Industry Trend and Market Intelligence.

## Outputs

- `market-data/market_data_report.json`
- `market-data/market_data_report.md`
- `market-data/official_universe.csv`
- `market-data/research_official.csv`
- `market-data/industry_price_history.csv`
- `market-data/fund_flow.csv`

## Quality Gate

The importer is `ready` only when every research stock has:

- an official TWSE or TPEx profile,
- an official industry name,
- at least 21 valid price observations.

Institutional-flow coverage is reported separately. Source errors, missing classifications, and short price histories remain explicit blockers.

## Trade-offs

- Monthly official endpoints are slower than a bulk paid feed, but remain public, auditable, and easy to fixture.
- TPEx volume is published in lots and is normalized to share-equivalent volume by multiplying by 1,000; this preserves within-stock volume ratios but should not be treated as exchange-certified share volume for non-standard securities.
- Official industry enrichment does not silently overwrite a user's analytical category. The official fields remain available beside the research taxonomy.
- The current importer produces a fresh batch snapshot. Scheduling and retained history remain v0.53 work.
