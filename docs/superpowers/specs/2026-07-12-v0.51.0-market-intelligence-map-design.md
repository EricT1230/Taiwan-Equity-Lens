# v0.51.0 Market Intelligence Industry Map Design

## Status

Accepted for implementation on `codex/v0.51.0-market-intelligence-map`.

## Context

v0.50.0 calculates deterministic 1D/5D/20D price and volume context from a local CSV. It does not ingest current news, extract research keywords, aggregate institutional fund flow, or prove that the three source families are fresh enough to read together. A complete industry map needs event, price, and capital-flow context without converting descriptive evidence into a recommendation engine.

## Decision

Keep the modular-monolith and batch-artifact architecture. Add a `market_intelligence` bounded context that:

- treats the research CSV as the explicit stock-to-industry taxonomy,
- adds `news_keywords` aliases for company names, products, technologies, and themes,
- accepts deterministic news and fund-flow CSV inputs,
- can fetch generic RSS/Atom feeds, TWSE News OpenAPI, and the TWSE T86 institutional flow report,
- maps news to stocks and industries with explicit aliases plus repeated-term extraction,
- aggregates foreign, investment-trust, dealer, and total net flow by research category,
- joins the existing Industry Trend Report by category,
- writes JSON, Markdown, and HTML snapshots with traceability metadata,
- exposes news, fund-flow, and price-trend freshness independently,
- renders Market Intelligence before the existing Industry Trend and Industry Map workflow.

The output remains descriptive research context. Positive price returns, news volume, and net inflow must never be converted into buy/sell/hold, attractiveness, target-price, or allocation labels.

## Alternatives Considered

### Add fields directly to `industry_trends.py`

This is initially smaller, but it couples three different update cadences and failure modes. A news outage should not invalidate price calculations, and a T86 holiday gap should remain distinguishable from missing price history.

### Add a database, scheduler, and background service now

This supports larger history and continuous ingestion, but adds operational state before the source contracts and freshness gates are stable. The batch snapshot remains easier to test, audit, and replace. Scheduling can call the stable CLI later.

### Use an LLM for all keyword and industry classification

This can improve recall, especially for emerging themes, but reduces determinism and requires a cost, privacy, and provenance contract. v0.51 uses explicit aliases and deterministic repeated-term extraction. A later optional classifier can add suggestions while preserving rule-based matches and source text.

## Bounded Contexts

- Research taxonomy: stock, company, industry, and explicit news aliases.
- Market trend: local price and volume history to descriptive rotation context.
- Event intelligence: source-normalized news, matched entities, and extracted keywords.
- Capital flow: stock-level institutional net flow aggregated to research industries.
- Quality gate: independent freshness, coverage, missingness, and next action.
- Presentation: generated report trio plus dashboard discovery and stable hooks.

## Data Contract

`market_intelligence_report.json` includes:

- `generated_at`, `run_metadata`, and `artifact_registry`,
- `freshness.news`, `freshness.fund_flow`, and `freshness.industry_trend`,
- `coverage` for mapped news, research-stock flow coverage, and industry coverage,
- `quality_gate` with explicit blockers,
- global `top_keywords`,
- `industries[]` containing market trend, latest mapped news, keywords, and institutional flow,
- normalized `news[]` and `fund_flows[]` evidence rows,
- `non_advice_notice`.

## Freshness Defaults

- News: 96 hours.
- Institutional flow: 5 calendar days, allowing weekends and short holidays.
- Industry price trend: 7 calendar days.

Missing and stale are different states. The combined report is `ready` only when all three source families are present and fresh and the news/flow evidence maps to the research universe.

## Evolution Path

1. v0.51: deterministic snapshot, official TWSE adapters, generic feeds, dashboard surface.
2. v0.52: official listed/OTC taxonomy importer and broader TWSE/TPEx flow coverage.
3. v0.53: scheduled incremental snapshots, history retention, deduplication, and source health metrics.
4. v0.54: optional semantic entity/theme suggestions with human confirmation and evaluation fixtures.
